Music used by Brainwave sessions.

  Global/    long beds played under a whole session (global_music line of a .seg)
  Segments/  sounds attached to one segment (its "music" key)

Paths inside this folder are saved relative to it, so a .seg opens on every
machine where the repository is cloned.

Provided (synthesised, just intonation on C, seamless loops, no noise):
  Global/nappe_nidra_evolutive.flac  180 s pad, C - Am - F - G, slow crossfades
  Segments/gong_lointain.flac        installation  - deep gong every 20 s (previous version)
  Segments/didgeridoo_gong_ancrage.flac installation - C didgeridoo drone (64 Hz) + distant gong
  Segments/didgeridoo_souffle.flac   breath        - didgeridoo opening 4 s / closing 6 s
  Segments/tingsha.flac              sankalpa      - tingsha pair every 15 s
  Segments/kalimba_lente.flac        rotation      - sparse pentatonic kalimba
  Segments/souffle_guide.flac        breath        - swell 4 s in / 6 s out (6 breaths/min)
  Segments/balayage_tiede.flac       sensations    - slow brightness sweep on C-E-G
  Segments/carillon_doux.flac        visualisation - sparse chimes
  Segments/harpe_glissando.flac      sankalpa      - soft harp glissando every 12.5 s
  Segments/eveil_montant.flac        return        - rising arpeggios
  Segments/gong_et_tingsha.flac      closing       - gong then three tingsha
